Venous Return Cannulae, Single Stage, Right Angle Lighthouse Tip, Wire-reinforced Tubing, 28 Fr, RV-41028

The Venous Return Cannula is indicated for use in drainage of the superior and inferior vena cava during cardiopulmonary bypass surgery. The Catheter consists of a flexible polyvinylchloride tubing wire reinforced tapered to a flow tip. The Catheters are available with a 90-degree bended distal section with a fluted distal flow tip.
