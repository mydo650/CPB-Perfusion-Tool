Support Material

Estimated Blood Volume
weight (kg) × blood volume factor (mL/kg)
Predicted Post-Prime Hct
(estimated blood volume × pre-CPB Hct) / (estimated blood volume + prime volume)
Hct Drop
pre-CPB Hct - predicted post-prime Hct
Prime : Blood Ratio
prime volume / estimated blood volume
PRBC Volume Needed
Exact mass-balance solution using target Hct, current red-cell mass, total diluted volume, and assumed PRBC hematocrit
Red Cell Deficit
Target red-cell volume after dilution - current red-cell volume before added PRBC

This tab assumes a clear prime and uses a configurable adult-default blood volume factor of 70 mL/kg. If your prime strategy or patient population differs, change the assumptions before using the result.

Prime calculations are decision-support only and do not replace local transfusion policy, product labeling, or clinician judgment.
