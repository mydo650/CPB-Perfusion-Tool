Support Material

BSA
√((height cm × weight kg) / 3600)
Recommended Flow Range
1.6 to 2.6 L/min/m² × BSA
DO2i
((Hgb × 1.34 × SaO2 fraction) + (0.003 × PaO2)) × 10 × effective cardiac index
Required CI / Flow / Hgb
Rearranged from the DO2i equation using the selected target and current oxygenation inputs

Variable definitions: Hgb = hemoglobin, SaO2 = arterial oxygen saturation, PaO2 = arterial oxygen tension, CI = cardiac index. If pump flow is entered, effective CI = pump flow / BSA.

Decision-support calculator for simulation use. Confirm protocol-specific targets and rounding requirements before clinical use. Aim to maintain DO2i above the selected target when clinically appropriate.
