Cannula Groups In Scope

Arterial perfusion cannulas
Central aortic, femoral or peripheral arterial, and pediatric arterial options that are used for forward perfusion support.
Venous drainage cannulas
Single-stage, dual-stage, multi-stage femoral venous, bicaval or two-stage venous, internal jugular venous, and pediatric venous options used for bypass drainage planning.
Patient and procedure factors
Body size, vessel size, target flow, access approach, procedure type, and expected drainage demands should all influence the selection workflow.

Interactive Chart Workflow

  1. Select a manufacturer family and cannula type.
  2. Select or compare one or more cannula sizes.
  3. Drag or set a target blood flow.
  4. Read the expected pressure drop at that flow from the published performance curve.
  5. Use the visual comparison to support selection and discussion during simulation planning.

Manufacturer Families To Build From

Medtronic Bio-Medicus NextGen
Strong first candidate because public product information includes pressure drop versus flow charts for femoral arterial, internal jugular venous, femoral venous multi-stage, femoral venous bi-caval, pediatric arterial, and pediatric venous models.
Getinge HLS Cannulae
Strong second candidate because the brochure includes arterial and venous HLS cannula pressure-drop curves across multiple sizes.
LivaNova adult and pediatric cannulae
Useful for later expansion because the portfolio clearly covers adult arterial, adult venous return, pediatric arterial, and pediatric venous categories.
