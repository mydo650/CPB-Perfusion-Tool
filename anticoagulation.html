    Support Material

    Heparin Loading Dose
    weight (kg) × heparin loading dose (units/kg)
    Estimated Blood Volume
    weight (kg) × EBV factor (mL/kg)
    Loading Concentration
    heparin units / estimated patient blood volume before bypass
    Bivalirudin Loading Dose
    weight (kg) × 1 mg/kg
    Argatroban Infusion Rate
    weight (kg) × 2 mcg/kg/min; argatroban is not approved for CPB/bypass anticoagulation
    AT3 Dose
    (desired AT3 - current AT3) × weight (kg) / 1.4
    HDR Rise/Run
    (post-heparin ACT - baseline ACT) / (loading heparin concentration - 0)
    AT3-Updated HDR
    (repeat ACT after AT3 - baseline ACT) / (loading heparin concentration - 0)
    Projected Concentration to Target
    (target ACT - baseline ACT) / HDR rise/run
    Protamine Reversal Dose
    (total logged heparin units / 100) × protamine ratio (mg per 100 units)

    The curve uses a simple linear ACT response between baseline ACT and the measured ACT after the first heparin dose. Before bypass, the loading heparin concentration is estimated from patient blood volume only; prime volume is displayed separately for on-bypass volume awareness after the circuit joins circulation. If a repeat ACT after AT3 is entered, the graph updates from the observed repeat ACT rather than predicting the AT3 effect from dose alone. The projection still requires local protocol review and clinical confirmation.

    Decision-support for simulation and workflow use only. Confirm local anticoagulation protocols and product labeling before clinical application.

    1. American Society of ExtraCorporeal Technology. AmSECT standards and guidelines for perfusion practice. AmSECT
    2. Default dosing assumptions are editable because anticoagulation strategies vary by institution, patient response, and clinician protocol.
    3. Shore-Lesserson L, Baker RA, Ferraris VA, et al. STS/SCA/AmSECT clinical practice guidelines on anticoagulation during CPB describe variable heparin responsiveness and limitations of dose-response prediction.
    4. Avidan MS, Levy JH, van Aken H, et al. Recombinant antithrombin restored heparin responsiveness in many heparin-resistant CPB patients, supporting repeat-ACT reassessment after AT3 correction.
