Support Material

Heparin Loading Dose
weight (kg) × heparin loading dose (units/kg)
Dose Response Slope
(post-heparin ACT - baseline ACT) / heparin dose (units/kg)
Projected Dose to Target
(target ACT - baseline ACT) / dose response slope
Protamine Reversal Dose
(estimated heparin units / 100) × protamine ratio (mg per 100 units)

The curve uses a simple linear ACT response between baseline ACT and the measured ACT after the test dose. It does not yet account for heparin concentration assays, supplemental bolus timing, heparin in the pump prime, or elapsed time since dosing.

Decision-support for simulation and workflow use only. Confirm local anticoagulation protocols and product labeling before clinical application.
